=====================================================
    MONEY DESTINATION TRACKING - Quick Guide
    BNI Enterprises - Bike Dealer Management System
=====================================================

WHAT IS IT?
-----------
After selling a bike, you can now track WHERE that sale money went:
- Bank (HBL, MCB, UBL, etc.)
- Person (owner, partner, manager)
- Wallet (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Cash Drawer)

This is OPTIONAL. You can sell bikes without tracking money.


WHERE TO FIND IT?
-----------------
1. SIDEBAR MENU:
   - "Money Destinations" (manage banks/persons/wallets)
   - "Money Tracking" (allocate sale money)

2. DURING A SALE:
   - At the bottom of the Sale form, expand "Track Money Destination"
   - Add rows: pick destination + amount
   - Saved with the sale automatically

3. INVENTORY PAGE:
   - Sold bikes show a cash icon button in actions column
   - Click it to go to Money Tracking for that bike

4. BIKE DETAIL VIEW:
   - View any sold bike's detail page
   - Scroll to "Money Destination Tracking" section
   - See cards: Sale Total, Allocated, Remaining
   - See table of all allocations
   - Click "Manage Allocations" button

5. REPORTS:
   - Go to Reports page
   - 4 new tabs at the end:
     * Money by Destination: totals per bank/person/wallet
     * Money by Sale: per-sale allocation breakdown
     * Untracked Sales: sales with missing allocations
     * Money Flow: monthly summary by destination type


HOW TO USE IT?
--------------
STEP 1: Set up destinations
   - Go to "Money Destinations" in sidebar
   - 9 defaults are already there (3 banks, 3 persons, 3 wallets)
   - Add/edit/delete as needed

STEP 2: Track money (Option A - During Sale)
   - When recording a new sale, expand "Track Money Destination"
   - Click "Add Destination Row"
   - Pick destination, enter amount
   - Add more rows if money goes to multiple places
   - Submit sale as usual

STEP 2: Track money (Option B - After Sale)
   - Go to "Money Tracking" in sidebar
   - Click "Add Allocation"
   - Select the sold bike, destination, amount, date
   - Save

STEP 3: Review
   - Check "Untracked Sales" in Reports to find sales you haven't tracked
   - Use "Money Flow" report for monthly analysis


KEY POINTS:
-----------
- One sale can split money to multiple destinations
- You can add/edit/delete allocations anytime after sale
- Deleting a sale automatically removes its allocations
- Remaining amount = Sale Total - Allocated (shown in yellow if > 0)
- Everything is permission-controlled (ask admin for access)
