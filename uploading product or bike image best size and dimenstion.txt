Best Image Dimensions for Uploads

To make the bikes look perfectly uniform and sharp on the landing page, stick to these guidelines:

Ideal Resolution: 800 x 600 pixels or 1200 x 900 pixels.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (Landscape).

Format: WebP or PNG with a transparent background looks best, but a solid white/black background works too.

Padding: Leave about 5-10% of empty space around the bike in the image so it doesn't touch the very edges of the frame.
